### 1. Overall Impressions of First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)
The **Workforce Bootcamp — First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program has been a highly practical and transformative internship experience. Rather than learning cloud concepts in isolation, FCAJ combines a real enterprise workspace at the 26th-Floor AWS Office in Bitexco, structured hands-on labs, vibrant community events, and rigorous production standards (Well-Architected Framework, IaC, Cost Optimization, Clean-up, and Live Demo). This environment pushed me to think and build like a production Cloud/Data Engineer.

---

### 2. Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Rating:** **Very Satisfied (5/5)**.
* **What Stood Out Most:**
  * Practical, candid mentorship and clear program direction from FCAJ Program Managers and Mentors.
  * A professional, disciplined office environment that builds workplace habits early in a student's career.
  * An active, supportive **AWS Study Group VN** community where students can publish real builds and receive constructive feedback from experienced practitioners.
  * Strong emphasis on **FinOps** (AWS Budgets, AWS Pricing Calculator, and `$0` teardown discipline), empowering students to build confidently on real AWS accounts without fear of surprise bills.

---

### 3. Suggestions for Improvement
* **Track-Specific Architecture Review / Office Hours:** Hosting mid-program architecture review clinics (around Weeks 6–8) tailored by track (such as *Data & Analytics*) would help interns catch design, IaC, and cost blind spots earlier before packaging their final Workshop.
* **More Modern Data Engineering & CloudOps Sample Labs:** Adding concise hands-on reference labs covering Redshift Serverless, Event-Driven SQS + DLQ patterns, and CloudFormation IaC alongside foundational labs would further accelerate Data-track learners.

---

### 4. Would You Recommend FCAJ to Peers? Why?
* **Absolutely yes!**
* **Why:** FCAJ equips IT students not only with practical AWS skills, but also with a **tangible, production-grounded engineering portfolio** (bilingual documentation site, live-verified Well-Architected project, community blog posts, and real tech event networking)—a tremendous advantage when entering the Cloud and Data job market.

My sincere thanks to the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) Organizing Team**, all **Mentors**, and the **AWS Study Group VN** community for guiding and supporting me throughout this internship!
