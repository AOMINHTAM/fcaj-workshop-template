### 1. Overall Reflection on First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)
The biggest takeaway for me after 3 months in **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** is realizing that I need to be much more proactive and self-driven in my learning. When working on real AWS accounts, nobody can walk you through every single step — you have to read docs, test things out, break them, fix them, and ask targeted questions when stuck.

---

### 2. What Stood Out Most
* **AWS Budgets and Billing Alarms:** This is definitely the habit that stuck with me the most. Because I was genuinely afraid of burning through my `$200` credit down to `$0`, I learned to set up budgets and billing alarms early, check pricing before spinning up services, and clean up resources right after testing.
* **People and Community:** The open atmosphere in the program made it easy to connect, chat, and learn from mentors and fellow interns.

---

### 3. Suggestions for Improvement
* **More In-Office Sessions:** I would love to see more scheduled in-office days at Bitexco where interns can work together in person. Being at the office makes it much easier to connect, ask quick questions, and stay focused compared to studying alone at home.

---

### 4. Would You Recommend FCAJ & Advice for Future Interns
* **Yes!** It is a great place for students to get hands-on experience on real AWS accounts and meet peers with similar interests.
* **Two practical tips for future batches:**
  1. **Watch your AWS Credits carefully:** Set up Budgets and Alarms on Day 1, and double-check that everything is deleted after you finish testing.
  2. **Always keep your own docs/notes:** Write down what you built, what broke, and how you fixed it as you go. Your future self will thank you when writing the final report or reviewing how your system works.
